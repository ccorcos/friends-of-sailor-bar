The Lt. Stephen D. Moore Flagpole Memorial is a dedicated landmark located at **Sailor Bar** along the American River in Fair Oaks, California.

## Memorial Details

- **Who It Honors:** First Lieutenant Stephen Douglas Moore, an Eagle Scout and Army infantry unit commander from Fair Oaks who lost his life during the Vietnam War on August 22, 1969.
- **Location:** Sailor Bar Park near the Olive Avenue entrance to the park.
- **Significance:** The site features a flagpole and a memorial plaque honoring his service and background as an Eagle Scout. Local community members and scout groups occasionally visit the site to perform maintenance or replace the American flag.
