## Launching on the American River: The Sailor Bar Boat Ramp in Fair Oaks

The **Sailor Bar Park Boat Ramp**, located off Illinois Avenue (4253 Illinois Ave, Fair Oaks, CA), serves as a **prime, less-crowded public access point** for small watercraft, rafts, kayaks, and paddleboards along the American River Parkway.

## On-the-Water Experience and Uses

The launch area provides direct, scenic access to a calmer, natural stretch of the American River. It is widely favored by locals looking to avoid the heavier congestion found at upstream locations like the Sunrise Recreation Area. Paddlers and tubers frequently use the Sailor Bar Park Boat Ramp to begin a relaxing float, short paddle, or fishing excursion. Anglers should note that this stretch of the river enforces specific regulations, such as barbless hook rules, and seasonal closures to protect native salmonids.

## Parking and Facilities

- **Location:** Situated at the main Sailor Bar Park entrance at the bottom of Illinois Avenue.
- **Parking Fees:** Sacramento County Regional Parks charges **$8 for standard vehicles** and **$15 for vehicles with a trailer** (such as a boat trailer) or RVs. Self-payment stations are available on-site if a fee collector is not present.
- **Amenities:** The site features convenient turnaround space for trailers, nearby portable restrooms, and direct connection points to surrounding park paths.
